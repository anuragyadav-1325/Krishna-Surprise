🌸 RADHA 🌸

Pure Love.
Pure Devotion.

✦

Radha represents pure love, devotion and a heart completely devoted to Krishna. Her love is remembered as a symbol of selfless devotion, faith and a beautiful spiritual bond.

"Some bonds are remembered not only for their beauty,
but for the devotion behind them."

💙 KRISHNA 💙

Love, Grace
& Divine Joy

✦

Krishna represents love, compassion, joy and divine grace. Together, Radha and Krishna are remembered as a beautiful symbol of devotion, harmony and an eternal spiritual bond.

"Some stories become timeless because the emotions
behind them never fade."
